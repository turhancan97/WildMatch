# Per-species example pairs

One individual per species, a query (test split) and a gallery (training split) photo, drawn by seeded random sampling (seed 0) from the paper's splits, except Leopard and Sea star, chosen by appearance (both photos show the same view; see `chosen` in species.csv), never by score, and taken from each dataset's original release (Wild Me photos cropped with the release's own box). The fire salamander pair is the one the project page shows.

- Leopard: Leopard ID 2022: Botswana Predator Conservation Trust (2022), African Carnivore Wildbook, via Wild Me and LILA BC, CDLA-Permissive-1.0.
- Spotted hyena: Hyena ID 2022: Botswana Predator Conservation Trust (2022), African Carnivore Wildbook, via Wild Me and LILA BC, CDLA-Permissive-1.0.
- Whale shark: Whale Shark ID: Holmberg, Norman and Arzoumanian (2009), Endangered Species Research 7, via Wild Me and LILA BC, CDLA-Permissive-1.0.
- Sea star: Sea Star Re-ID 2023: Wahltinez and Wahltinez (2024), Methods in Ecology and Evolution, via LILA BC, CDLA-Permissive-1.0.
- Sea turtle: Turtle Recall Conservation Challenge (Zindi, Local Ocean Conservation and Google DeepMind), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Fire salamander: SalamanderID2025 (AnimalCLEF 2025), AnimalCLEF2025 rules; shown with the organisers' permission (non-commercial).

Modified: cropped and resized to 640 px. The sea turtle photos stay under CC BY-SA 4.0.
